COUNT I — FRAUD AND MISREPRESENTATION

15. Plaintiff realleges and incorporates all preceding paragraphs.

16. Defendants intentionally made material misrepresentations regarding Meridian's financial
health, including falsified revenue figures totaling $94,200,000 in fiscal year 2022.

17. Harrington, relying on these misrepresentations, transferred $47,500,000.00 as described
above. As a direct result, Harrington has suffered damages of not less than $47,500,000.00, plus
interest at the rate of 9% per annum from the date of breach.

COUNT II — BREACH OF FIDUCIARY DUTY

18. Delacroix and Westbrook, as officers of Meridian, owed fiduciary duties to Harrington as
an investor. By diverting corporate funds and engaging in self-dealing transactions, they breached
those duties, causing additional damages of approximately $1,604,212.00.

COUNT III — COMPUTER FRAUD AND ABUSE ACT (18 U.S.C. § 1030)

19. Westbrook's unauthorized access to Meridian's protected computer systems from IP address
203.0.113.87 and IPv6 address 2001:0db8:85a3:0000:0000:8a2e:0370:7334 constitutes a violation
of the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act.
