F. EXPERT OPINION SUMMARY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

In my opinion, the care provided by Mr. Victor Osei-Bonsu and the clinical team at Hammersmith
Hospital on 17–20 March 2022 fell below the standard of a reasonably competent consultant
cardiac surgeon. Specifically:

1. The decision to proceed with PCI without pre-operative CT angiography represents a departure
   from NICE Guideline NG185 (issued 18 November 2020).

2. The retroperitoneal haemorrhage was a foreseeable consequence of the anticoagulation
   protocol adopted, and a more conservative dosing regimen should have been employed.

3. Had Mrs. Callahan (NHS 943 476 5600) received the standard of care expected, on the
   balance of probabilities she would not have sustained the sequelae described.

I confirm that I understand my duty to the Court and I have complied with that duty. I confirm
